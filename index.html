Athletic Eligibility Clearinghouses

High school athletes planning to compete in college must register with official eligibility centers by their junior year.

NCAA Eligibility Center

Official clearinghouse for Division 1 and Division 2 student-athletes. Tracks core high school course requirements, GPA, and amateurism certification.
  • Requirements: 16 Core Courses (English, Math, Science, Social Science)
  • Target Registration: 10th or 11th Grade
  • SAT/ACT Code: 9999
Register with NCAA Clearinghouse

NAIA Eligibility Center (PlayNAIA)

Official clearinghouse for all NAIA athletic competition. Allows student-athletes to create an online recruiting profile viewable by 250+ NAIA coaches.
  • Requirements: 2.0+ GPA, Top 50% Class Rank, or Qualifying ACT/SAT
  • Platform: PlayNAIA Recruiting & Eligibility Hub
  • Benefits: Direct connection to NAIA program head coaches
Register with PlayNAIA

Colleges & Universities Directory by Level

Explore competition levels, scholarship structures, and member institutional directories across collegiate basketball.

NCAA Division I (D1)

The highest level of collegiate athletics with maximum national TV exposure, elite facilities, and multi-million dollar athletic budgets.
  • Scholarships: Full Athletic Scholarships (Head Count Sport: 13 per team)
  • Competition: Premier national travel, Power 4 & Mid-Major Conferences
  • Programs: 350+ Division I Basketball Institutions
Browse D1 Colleges Directory

NCAA Division II (D2)

Highly competitive regional athletics offering partial-to-full athletic scholarship packages combined with strong academic focus.
  • Scholarships: Partial Athletic Scholarships (Equivalency Model: 10 fulls divided)
  • Competition: High-level regional competition with national post-season play
  • Programs: 300+ Division II Institutions
Browse D2 Colleges Directory

NCAA Division III (D3)

Academics-first model featuring the largest NCAA division. Offers fast-paced competition with high emphasis on degree completion.
  • Scholarships: Academic Merit Aid & Need-Based Grants (No Athletic Scholarships)
  • Competition: Intense regional leagues with reduced off-season travel
  • Programs: 440+ Division III Institutions
Browse D3 Colleges Directory

NAIA Athletics

Small to mid-sized private and public colleges offering high-level competition comparable to top NCAA D2 and D3 programs.
  • Scholarships: Full and Partial Athletic Scholarships available
  • Flexibility: Custom eligibility rules and direct coach communications
  • Programs: 250+ NAIA Member Institutions
Browse NAIA Member Directory

JUCO / NJCAA (Junior College)

2-Year college athletic pathway ideal for players gaining varsity experience, building recruiting film, or raising academic GPAs before transferring.
  • Scholarships: Full Tuition, Housing, & Partial Athletic Aid (Divisions I & II)
  • Transfer Track: Direct 2-year path into 4-year NCAA D1/D2/NAIA programs
  • Programs: 500+ NJCAA Member Colleges
Browse JUCO Member Directory
